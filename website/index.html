Preserve first

Original RFC 5322 bytes remain canonical. Search databases and interface views are rebuildable derivatives.

Search quickly

Stable mid-#### identifiers, indexed full-text search, date rules, and provenance make old mail easy to revisit.

03

Keep it inspectable

BagIt metadata, SHA-256 fixity, source locations, and documented recovery rules keep the archive understandable.
