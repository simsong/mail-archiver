The repository keeps these reports as source-controlled working documents:

- [Competitive analysis](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/competitive_analysis.md)—preservation, migration, search, and compliance tools.
- [Project direction](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/project_direction.md)—principles, non-goals, and the roadmap.
- [Data-quality audit](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/DATA_QUALITY_AUDIT.md)—diagnostic methods for long-lived collections.
- [Integrity controls](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/INTEGRITY_CONTROLS.md)—BagIt, Mailbag, and message-level fixity.
- [Email servers research](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/Email%20Servers.md)—read-only source and provider considerations.

These are evolving project documents, not a claim that every described
capability is already implemented. The [README](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/README.md)
and [release notes](https://github.com/simsong/mail-archiver/blob/main/doc/RELEASE_NOTES.md)
separate current behavior from planned work.
