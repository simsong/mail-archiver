title = "Organizations and communities"
description = "Organizations whose guidance helps shape digital email curation."
+++

- [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/digital/)
  publishes practical digital-preservation guidance and resources.
- [Digital Preservation Coalition](https://www.dpconline.org/)
  brings together institutions and practitioners working on digital continuity.
- [Harvard Library](https://preservation.library.harvard.edu/digital-preservation)
  documents institutional digital-preservation practice.
- [ePADD](https://epaddproject.org/)
  develops open-source tools for processing, discovering, and delivering email
  archives.
- [The International Internet Preservation Consortium](https://netpreserve.org/)
  coordinates preservation practice across memory institutions and national
  libraries.

These links are starting points for further reading. Mail Archiver does not
represent or speak for any of these organizations.
